Cookie policy

We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and we don't load anything from advertising networks or social media. Our fonts are served from our own website, not from a third party.

The app keeps a few items in your browser's storage (local storage) that are strictly necessary for it to work. Because they are essential, the law doesn't require us to ask for consent first. That's why you won't see a cookie banner. They never leave your device except to sign you in to our own servers.

What we store on your device

NamePurposeHow long
sb-…-auth-tokenKeeps you signed in (set by our authentication provider, Supabase).Until you log out
slc_modeRemembers which Discover tab you used last (Worldwide, Near me…).Until you clear it
Offline cacheApp files saved by our service worker so the app opens quickly and works on a weak connection.Replaced with each update
slc_db_v2, slc_session_v1Demo mode only: the sample data and demo sign-in, kept in your browser.Until you clear it

Your choices

You can clear this storage at any time in your browser's settings (for example "Clear site data"). You'll be signed out, and the app will work again the next time you sign in. Logging out also removes your sign-in session.

If we ever add optional cookies (for example analytics), we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
