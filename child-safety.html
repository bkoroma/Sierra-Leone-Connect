Child safety standards

Sierra Leone Connect is an adults-only (18+) dating service. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

What we prohibit

  • Any account belonging to a person under 18.
  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers a minor, including images, text, voice recordings or links.
  • Grooming: any attempt to contact, befriend or arrange to meet a minor, or to use the service to reach children.
  • Soliciting, sharing or trading CSAM in any form.

How we prevent it

  • A date of birth is required at sign-up and checked again on our servers; under-18s cannot create an account.
  • On Google Play the app is restricted to users aged 18 and over.
  • Members can report any profile or message from inside the app; "Underage" is a dedicated report reason.
  • Moderators review reports, optional selfie verification and automatically flagged messages.

How we respond

  • When we become aware of CSAE or CSAM we remove the content, permanently ban the account and preserve the evidence required by law.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, and to the relevant national authority where required.
  • We cooperate with law-enforcement requests made through proper legal process.

Report a concern

In the app: open the profile or message → Report → Underage.
By email to our child safety point of contact: safety email.
If a child is in immediate danger, contact local police first (US 911 · UK 999 · Sierra Leone 999 / 019), or report to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org.

Our designated child safety contact can speak to our enforcement and review practices and act on reports.
