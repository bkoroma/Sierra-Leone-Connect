Terms & community rules

By using Sierra Leone Connect you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the service.

Who can join

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • One account per person, with your own real photos and accurate details.
  • You must not be barred from using the service by law, or previously banned by us.

Community rules

Treat everyone the way you would want your own family to be treated. These are not allowed and lead to removal of content, suspension or a permanent ban:

  • Asking for or offering money, gift cards, crypto, "investments", or fees for transport, visas, school or hospital.
  • Fake profiles, impersonation, or photos that are not you.
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech or bullying — including about tribe, region, faith, gender or nationality.
  • Nudity, sexual content, or sexual solicitation.
  • Any content involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children, or any attempt to contact a minor. This results in an immediate permanent ban and a report to the authorities. See our child safety standards.
  • Spam, advertising, or promoting other services.
  • Sharing someone else's private information without consent.

Reporting and moderation

Use Report on any profile or message. Reports are confidential and reviewed by our moderators. We may review flagged messages to keep members safe. We can remove content or accounts that break these rules, at our discretion.

Your content

You own what you post. You give us permission to store and show it to other members so the service works. You are responsible for what you share and for your interactions with other members.

Meeting people

We don't run criminal background checks. Meet in public, tell someone you trust where you're going, and never send money to someone you haven't met. Read our safety tips in the app.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time (Profile → Delete account, or on the web). We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

Disclaimers

The service is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for the conduct of members on or off the service.
