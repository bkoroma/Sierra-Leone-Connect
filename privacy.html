Privacy policy

Sierra Leone Connect ("we", "us") is a dating service for Sierra Leoneans and friends of Salone around the world. It is operated by Sierra Leone Connect, . We are the "controller" of your personal data. Questions or requests: support email.

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run a safe dating service. We never sell your data, share it for advertising, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Other members see your username, never your full name or exact location.
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Settings).

What we collect

  • Account: first and last name, username, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), date of birth, gender and country.
  • Profile you choose to share: photos, a voice intro, bio, prompt answers, job, education, height, home district, heritage, languages, interests, relationship goal and who you want to see.
  • Compatibility answers: your answers to optional questions about values and lifestyle (for example faith, children, marriage and moving home).
  • Location: when you sign up your device can share its location to fill in your city and country; if you decline we use your device's time zone. We store coordinates rounded to about 1 km. Other members only see your city and an approximate distance.
  • Activity: likes and passes, matches, messages and voice notes, reports and blocks, and when you were last active.
  • Verification selfie (optional): used only by a moderator to confirm your photos are you, and deleted as soon as the decision is made.
  • Consent records: which version of these terms you accepted and when.
  • Technical data: if the app crashes, it sends us the error message, the page, the app version and your browser type, linked to your account if you're signed in. Our hosting providers also keep standard server logs (including IP addresses) for security.

Sensitive information

Some details on a dating profile can reveal sensitive information — for example your faith, your ethnic or cultural heritage, and who you are interested in (which can indicate your sexual orientation). We use these only to show you compatible matches, and only with your explicit consent, which you give when you create your account. Faith and compatibility answers are optional. You can remove them from your profile at any time, or withdraw consent by deleting your account.

How we use it, and our legal bases

  • To provide the service — show profiles, calculate compatibility, deliver messages (performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep members safe — age checks, Scam Shield (automatic flagging of messages asking for money, gift cards or crypto, or moving you off the app), reviewing reports, verification and enforcing our rules (our legitimate interest in a safe community, and legal obligations).
  • To use sensitive details for matching (your explicit consent).
  • To fix problems — crash reports and logs (legitimate interest in a working, secure service).
  • To send account emails — sign-up confirmation and password resets. We do not send marketing emails.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. Scam Shield only flags messages for a human moderator to review.

Who processes your data

We share data only with service providers who process it on our instructions:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage and real-time chat (servers in the United States).
  • Render — hosts the website files (United States).
  • Our email provider — sends account emails.
  • Google Play — distributes the Android app under Google's own privacy policy.

We may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm — including reporting child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant authority.

International transfers: our providers are in the United States. Where data protection law requires it (for example for people in the UK or EU), transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, voice recordings, answers, likes, matches and messages are erased immediately. Copies in encrypted backups expire within 30 days.
  • Verification selfies: deleted when a moderator decides.
  • Crash reports: 30 days.
  • Moderation records: a record that an action happened (for example "account deleted"), without your email or username. If an account was banned for breaking our rules, we keep a one-way scrambled code (a hash) of its email address so the same person can't simply sign up again.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: Profile → Settings → Download my data gives you a copy of everything we hold about you.
  • Correction: edit your profile at any time.
  • Deletion: Profile → Settings → Delete account, or on the web at delete-account.html.
  • Withdraw consent / object: remove optional details from your profile, pause your profile, or delete your account.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the UK, EU, or US states such as California) you may have further rights, including to complain to your data protection authority. Email support and we will respond within one month.
  • California: we do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to provide the service.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The app stores a few things on your device that it needs to work, such as your sign-in session. See our cookie policy.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request is permission-checked on our servers, staff tools require two-factor authentication, and every moderator action is logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Age

Sierra Leone Connect is for adults 18 and over only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete accounts that we find belong to minors. See our child safety standards.

Changes

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app and ask you to review them before you continue.
