Privacy policy

Sierra Leone Connect ("we") is run by Sierra Leone Connect. This policy explains what we collect when you use our website and Android app, why, and the choices you have. Contact: support email.

What we collect

  • Account: first and last name, username, email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), date of birth and country. Other members only see your username; your first and last name are private (visible to you and our moderators).
  • Profile you choose to share: first name, gender and who you want to see, photos, a voice intro, bio, prompts, job, education, height, home district, heritage, languages, interests and relationship goals.
  • Faith and values: your faith and your answers to compatibility questions (for example about children, marriage and moving home). These can be sensitive; you choose whether to answer, and they are used only for matching.
  • Location: when you sign up we ask your device for its location to fill in your country and city (if you decline, we use your device's time zone instead). This happens on your device, without third-party location services, and we store coordinates rounded to about 1 km. Other members only ever see your city and an approximate distance.
  • Activity: likes and passes, matches, messages and voice notes, reports and blocks, and when you were last active.
  • Verification selfie (optional): seen only by our moderators, never shown on your profile.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show profiles, calculate compatibility, deliver messages.
  • To keep members safe: age checks, Scam Shield (automatic flagging of messages that ask for money, gift cards, crypto or move you off the app), reviewing reports, and verification.
  • To send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset.

We do not sell your data, use it for third-party advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage and real-time chat (servers in the United States).
  • Our web host — serves the website files.
  • Our email provider — sends account emails.

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm, including reporting child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant authority.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Data access is checked on the server for every request, staff tools require two-factor authentication, and every moderator action is logged.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on the web at delete-account.html. Deletion permanently erases your profile, photos, voice recordings, answers, likes, matches and messages. We keep a minimal moderation record (an anonymous ID and the action taken) to enforce bans and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, download a copy of your data or ask us to correct or delete it by emailing support. Depending on where you live (for example the UK, EU or California) you may have further rights, which we will honour.

Age

Sierra Leone Connect is for adults 18 and over only. We remove accounts we find belong to anyone younger.

Changes

If we make important changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
